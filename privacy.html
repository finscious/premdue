Privacy policy

PremDue by Finscious · last updated 11 November 2026

We collect nothing

PremDue has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and it does not use the internet. We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal data about you or your customers.

Your customers' data stays on your phone

Due lists you open, customer names, policy numbers, phone numbers you link, call outcomes, reminders and plan presentations are stored only on your phone, in PremDue's private storage.

  • Contacts: if you allow it, PremDue reads your contacts on your phone to suggest a customer's number. Contacts are never copied off your phone.
  • Notifications: used only for the daily 9 AM summary of your dues.
  • Sharing: when you tap Call, SMS, WhatsApp or Share, PremDue hands the number or file to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Backups

Backups go only to places you control: Android's automatic backup to your own Google account (only when your phone can encrypt it end to end), and backup files you choose to save, for example to your Google Drive. We cannot read them.

Deleting your data

Because nothing reaches us, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling PremDue, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of its data from your phone. Delete any backup files you saved wherever you saved them.

Version 1.0.0 of PremDue, distributed before November 2026, used Google sign-in for a trial and kept your Google account ID, email address and trial or payment status on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai. To have that record erased, write to us at the address below.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us about any data we hold, or ask us to correct or erase it, and raise a grievance, by writing to ceo.finscious@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Not affiliated with LIC

PremDue is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Life Insurance Corporation of India.
