Privacy policy

PremDue · last updated 25 October 2026

Your customers' data stays with you

Due lists, customer names, policy numbers, phone numbers you link, reminders and presentations are stored on your phone. PremDue never sends them to us. Contacts are read on your phone, only after you allow it, to suggest numbers.

Backups go only to places you control: Android's automatic backup to your own Google account (only when your phone can encrypt it end to end), and backup files you choose to save, for example to your Google Drive. We cannot read them.

What we collect

  • Your Google account ID and email address, when you sign in.
  • The date your free trial started, and whether you have paid (with the Razorpay payment reference).

We use this only to run your 7-day trial and your ₹299 purchase. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or share it, except with Razorpay to process your payment. It is stored on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, India.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or raise a grievance, by writing to ceo.finscious@gmail.com. Uninstalling PremDue deletes all data on your phone.
